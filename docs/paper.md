# Paper and citation

netlogopy was presented at the **Operational Research Society Simulation
Workshop 2025 (SW25)**, Exeter, United Kingdom, March 2025.

> Nourddine Bouaziz, Belgacem Bettayeb, M'Hammed Sahnoun, Adnan Yassine.
> *Netlogopy: Unlocking Advanced Simulation and Integration for NetLogo Using
> Python.* SW25.
> [doi:10.36819/SW25.019](https://doi.org/10.36819/SW25.019) -
> [hal-04982069](https://hal.science/hal-04982069) -
> [PDF](https://github.com/bouaziznourddine/netlogopy/blob/main/paper/netlogopy-SW25-paper.pdf)

The paper describes netlogopy's goals (NetLogo's visual agent-based modelling
together with Python's libraries for optimisation and AI), its functions
(agents, movement, links, background images, simulation management) and its
integration with existing NetLogo models, and presents an application to a job
shop with human operators.

The code printed in the paper runs as it is with netlogopy 1.0, including its
import spelling `from netlogopy.nelogopy import *`; the test suite executes
the listings of sections 2.3.1 to 2.3.7.
